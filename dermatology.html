Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding dermatology care and skin services at Royall Clinic in Klang.

Do I need an advanced appointment for a skin consultation?

While we do accept walk-in patients depending on daily slot availability, we strongly recommend scheduling a consultation slot via WhatsApp ahead of time to minimize waiting times and secure an dedicated evaluation slot with our doctor.

What types of acne do you treat at your Klang clinic?

We provide medical-grade solutions for structural forms of acne, including persistent cystic acne, hormonal blemishes, comedonal acne, and inflammatory pustules. Our care paths involve prescription topical therapies and oral medications when clinically indicated.

Can you assist with long-standing chronic eczema flare-ups?

Yes. We focus heavily on symptom control and skin barrier barrier repair during aggressive flares. Our medical practitioners provide potent anti-inflammatory topicals, non-irritating barrier moisturizers, and strategic advice on managing environmental or nutritional triggers.

Are your clinical skin treatments suitable for children?

Absolutely. We manage standard pediatric skin situations, such as infant eczema, heat rashes, contact diaper dermatitis, and contagious viral or fungal skin infections, using gentle, age-appropriate medical applications.

How long does it typically take to see visible results from treatments?

Skin turnaround varies widely based on cellular cycles and severity. Acute infections or minor allergic reactions can resolve within a few days of starting treatment. Chronic conditions like hormonal acne or pigmentation usually take 4 to 8 weeks of consistent therapeutic care to show structural improvements.
