GP Care Services

Practical care from a medical professional

Royall Clinic provides practical GP care for patients who need diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, referrals, or reassurance from a medical professional.

Acute Illness

Consultation for fever, cough, flu, sore throat, stomach upset, headache, dizziness, body aches, and minor infections.

Chronic Disease

Ongoing review for diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, asthma, gout, and long-term medication needs.

Medication Review

Doctor guidance for current prescriptions, side effects, dosage questions, and safe continuation of treatment.

Family Healthcare

Care for adults, elderly patients, children, and families who need regular clinic support.

Medical Certificates

Assessment-based medical certificates and documentation where clinically appropriate.

Referral Guidance

Doctor advice when specialist referral, imaging, blood tests, or further investigation is needed.

When To Visit

Come in early when symptoms persist

Early review can help clarify what is happening, reduce complications, and guide safe treatment choices.

Useful reasons to book

  • Symptoms lasting more than a few days or getting worse.
  • Repeated fever, breathing discomfort, dehydration, or severe pain.
  • Blood pressure, sugar, or cholesterol follow-up.
  • Questions about medication, test results, or next steps.
  • Need for vaccination, screening, or preventive care advice.
