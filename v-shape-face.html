Frequently Asked Questions

Essential clinical details regarding V-shape treatment durations, experiences, and maintenance.

How long do the results of a non-surgical V-shape face treatment last?

Masseter jaw slimming results generally last around 4 to 6 months as muscle activity slowly returns. Chin projection treatments utilizing structural fillers are longer-lasting, maintaining their streamlined profile for 12 to 18 months.

Is there any downtime after the face slimming procedure?

There is virtually no downtime. Most clients return to their regular daily routines immediately. You might experience slight tenderness or mild redness around the entry sites, which naturally fades over a few hours.

When will I start to see a visibly slimmer jawline?

Chin fillers show immediate definition right after the session. For jaw slimming injections, you will notice a gradual softening and narrowing of the lower face over 2 to 4 weeks as the muscle relaxes into its slimmer shape.
