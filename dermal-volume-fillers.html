Frequently Asked Questions

Does filler treatment hurt?

Most patients report only mild discomfort. A topical numbing agent is often used and many filler formulas already contain local anesthetic.

Will I have downtime?

There is usually little to no downtime. Mild swelling or bruising may be temporary and usually settles within a few days.

Can filler help with cheek and jawline enhancement?

Yes. Many patients choose dermal fillers for cheek volume, contouring, and jawline definition to restore balance and structure without invasive procedures.

Can filler help deepen wrinkles?

Yes, especially static wrinkles caused by volume loss. Fillers can soften folds and restore structure while supporting surrounding tissues.
