Commonly Affected Areas

This chronic variant of eczema presents predominantly where sebaceous (oil-producing) glands maintain the highest concentrations:

  • The Scalp Line: Spanning from minor, continuous dandruff flakes to highly adherent, thick yellow scales.
  • Facial Symmetry: Targeting sensitive eyebrow zones, eyelids (blepharitis), and nasolabial nose folds.
  • Chest & Upper Back: Showing up as greasy, highly irritated circular plaques across the chest.
  • Ear Channels: Provoking inflammation within the external canals and post-auricular skin fold zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find straightforward medical insights regarding the clinical resolution of seborrheic dermatitis.

Is seborrheic dermatitis completely curable?

While seborrheic dermatitis is a chronic condition, it can be managed into long-term, asymptomatic remission with professional medical care. Our goal is to clear active flares and prevent them from returning.

Does weather or climate impact this skin condition?

Yes. The high humidity and warmth across Malaysia can encourage oil production and yeast multiplication. Our treatments are specifically tailored to protect your skin in tropical climates.

Can seborrheic dermatitis cause hair loss?

Severe, untreated scalp inflammation and vigorous scratching can temporarily weaken hair follicles, leading to thinning. Resolving the underlying inflammation quickly halts this process and allows hair to recover.
