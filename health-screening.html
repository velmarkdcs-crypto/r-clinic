Screening FAQ

Common questions about health screening

Helpful answers to questions patients often ask before booking a screening at Royall Clinic.

Do I need to fast before my screening?

Many blood test profiles require 8 to 10 hours of fasting. Our team will advise you when you book so you can prepare correctly.

How long does a screening take?

Most basic screenings take around 20 to 30 minutes. Larger packages with additional tests or examinations may take longer.

When will my results be ready?

Most routine results are available within a few working days. The doctor will arrange a review to explain them in plain language.

Can I use screening for work or insurance?

Yes. We support pre-employment checks and can provide documentation for workplace, corporate, and administrative requirements.

What if something abnormal is found?

The doctor will explain the finding, suggest monitoring or treatment, and refer you to a specialist when further investigation is needed.
