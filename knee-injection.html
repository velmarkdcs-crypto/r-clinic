Addressing Knee Osteoarthritis Effectively

As knee joints age or sustain repetitive pressure, the natural synovial fluid that lubricates cartilage begins to dry out and thin. This progressive loss of lubrication leads directly to stiffness, clicking noises during movement, and ongoing pain during basic daily walks around Klang.

Relying exclusively on systemic oral pain remedies can overwork your digestive system and fail to resolve the core structural friction issue. Our specialized clinical knee injections deliver target fluids directly into the joint space, helping cushion your movements and improve your quality of life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Accurate clinical answers regarding our knee injection treatment pathways.

What type of knee injections are available at Royall Clinic?

We provide targeted intra-articular injections tailored to individual joint conditions. This includes Hyaluronic Acid (often called gel or viscosupplementation injections) to restore natural lubrication for osteoarthritis, as well as specialized anti-inflammatory options to ease acute swelling and localized pain.

Are knee injections painful, and is there any recovery downtime?

The procedure causes minimal discomfort. Our clinical team applies a localized numbing agent prior to administering the treatment. There is no major downtime required; however, we advise patients to avoid high-impact physical exercises or heavy lifting for 48 hours post-injection.

How quickly will I notice pain relief, and how long does it last?

Anti-inflammatory compounds generally alleviate intense joint pain within 2 to 7 days. Hyaluronic Acid gel options function gradually, reaching optimal therapeutic mechanical efficiency over a few weeks, with results consistently offering relief for 6 to 12 months depending on the stage of joint degradation.

Am I a suitable candidate for intra-articular gel injections?

Gel injections work best for individuals suffering from mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis (Stages 1 to 3) who have not found adequate relief through oral medications or routine physical therapy. Our in-house medical doctor will complete a physical assessment to confirm eligibility.
