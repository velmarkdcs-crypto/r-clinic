Our Minor Wound Interventions

When soft tissue integrity is disrupted, our clinical setup provides sterile, rapid, and precise closure mechanisms to secure margins and minimize scar formation.

Interrupted Suturing

Classic high-tensile medical stitching ideal for moving areas, ensuring individual knot security across skin lacerations and jagged trauma margins.

Subcuticular Closure

Intradermal running stitches placed entirely beneath the outermost skin profile. Perfect for maximizing cosmetic results on facial or highly visible cuts.

Surgical Gluing / Strips

Non-invasive skin adhesives applied meticulously for superficial, straight-edged cuts with minimal tension, offering zero stitch-removal downtime.

Critical Triage Indicators

Certain wounds cannot be managed within an outpatient clinic structure. Seek immediate hospital accident emergency departments if you experience:

  • Uncontrolled or spurting bright red arterial blood flow.
  • Loss of sensation, motor numbness, or tingling past the injury zone.
  • Wounds exposing visible underlying bone, muscle tissue, or tendons.
  • Deep chest, abdominal wall, or neck piercing trauma.
  • Animal or human bite wounds with high anaerobic contamination risk.

Why Professional Treatment Matters

Ignoring open cuts or applying home bandages over unsterilized tissue can lead to serious systemic health issues:

  • Infection Mitigation: Surgical debridement removes trapped rust, soil, and debris to stop cellulitis development.
  • Tetanus Prophylaxis: Access to timely booster assessments for deep, contaminated metal or soil punctures.
  • Optimal Cosmetics: Aligning skin edges cleanly avoids irregular keloid formation and uneven healing.

The Closure Process

How our medical team ensures a sterile, pain-free, and safe wound care treatment experience.

Step 01

Inspection

The doctor gauges the depth, checks nerve responses, and looks for foreign bodies or hidden tissue damage.

Step 02

Local Numbing

Infiltration of local anaesthetic directly around the margins to achieve complete pain relief before washing.

Step 03

Sterile Flushing

High-pressure irrigation with medical saline to thoroughly remove micro-contaminants and loose tissue cells.

Step 04

Stitching & Care

The margins are carefully closed under zero tension, layered with antibiotic barriers, and safely wrapped.
