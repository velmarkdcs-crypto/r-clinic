Permanent Excision for Benign Fatty Lumps

A lipoma is a benign, slow-growing growth of fatty tissue that forms comfortably beneath your skin layers. While generally non-cancerous and painless, they can gradually expand over time, placing undue pressure on adjacent nerves or altering your physical silhouette. Draining or altering lipomas at home is not possible. Our medical team offers sterile, fast-performing minor surgical procedures designed to safely extract the complete fatty tissue accumulation, eliminating the lump completely.

When Should Lipomas Be Removed?

Though optional, it is recommended to consider surgical extraction if you note any of the following conditions:

  • The mass is growing larger or feels increasingly prominent.
  • It causes physical pain or restricts movement in joints.
  • The lump becomes cosmetically distressing or visibly obvious.
  • It feels firm or loses its characteristic soft, moveable feel.

Frequently Asked Questions About Lipoma Removal

Clear, direct insights from our clinical practitioners regarding minor surgery in Klang.

Is lipoma removal painful?

Not at all. The entire minor surgery is done under local anaesthesia. You will feel a minor prick during the initial numbing phase, but the actual tissue excision is completely painless.

Will there be a scar after lipoma excision?

Any surgical incision leaves a mark, but our doctors apply advanced plastic and micro-suturing techniques to keep the incision line as minimal and clean as possible.

How long does the recovery take?

Most patients return to office duties immediately. The local skin wound typically closes up within 7 to 10 days, after which you will drop by our clinic for suture removal.

Can a lipoma return after it's extracted?

Once a lipoma is fully extracted via surgical excision, it rarely returns to that exact spot. However, individuals prone to lipomas may occasionally form separate lumps elsewhere over time.
