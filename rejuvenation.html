Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear, direct insights regarding non-surgical hand rejuvenation and anti-aging treatments at our Klang clinic.

What causes hands to age faster, and how does rejuvenation help?

The skin on the back of the hands is exceptionally thin and constantly exposed to harsh environmental elements, detergents, and UV radiation. Over time, UV damage triggers sun spots, while natural fat loss causes veins, bones, and tendons to appear more prominent. Rejuvenation therapies replace lost volume, break down pigment, and rebuild collagen structural layers.

How long do the results of dermal hand fillers last?

Dermal fillers designed to correct volume loss in the hands generally yield instant results that last between 12 to 18 months, depending on your lifestyle and the exact structural formula chosen by our doctor.

Is there extensive downtime after a medical hand peeling or laser session?

Downtime is completely manageable. Laser pigment removal or medical peeling causes mild redness and temporary darkening of sun spots, which flake away naturally within 5 to 7 days, leaving smoother, clear skin.

Is the treatment painful?

Comfort is a key priority. We use high-potency topical numbing creams prior to any injection or laser procedure, and our premium dermal filler options contain integrated local anesthetics to ensure a highly comfortable experience.
