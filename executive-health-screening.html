Patient Fasting Guidelines

To ensure accurate diagnostic processing, please observe these instructions:

  • Fast completely from all food and caloric liquids for 8 to 12 hours before your visit.
  • Drinking plain, pure water is allowed and encouraged to keep your veins well-hydrated.
  • Postpone taking routine morning diabetic medications until after your blood collection.
  • Bring along past clinical test reports to provide comparative trends for our medical team.
