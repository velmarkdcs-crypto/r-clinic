Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding medical procedures and patient timelines.

How long do I need to wait after exposure before getting an STD test?

Different STIs have varying window periods. For example, accurate Chlamydia and Gonorrhea testing typically requires 2 to 14 days post-exposure, while HIV and Syphilis blood tests are most reliable after 4 to 12 weeks. Our medical team in Klang will advise you on the correct timeline during your consultation.

Is my sexual health screening completely confidential at Royall Clinic?

Yes, patient privacy is our absolute priority. All consultations, physical examinations, laboratory test options, and treatment workflows are strictly confidential under standard Malaysian healthcare privacy guidelines.

How long does it take to receive standard STI screening test results?

Rapid tests (like finger-prick blood markers for HIV or Syphilis) often provide preliminary data within 15 to 20 minutes during your visit. Comprehensive clinical laboratory panels usually return definitive answers within 2 to 4 working days.

What happens if an infectious marker returns positive?

Most common bacterial STIs can be entirely resolved with targeted antibiotics. If a viral marker is detected, our clinic offers standard primary management pathways, prescription counseling, and direct specialist support to safeguard your wellness.
