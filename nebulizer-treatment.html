Clinic-Based Nebuliser Therapy in Klang

Direct, fast relief when breathing becomes difficult — administered by a doctor in a comfortable, ventilated room.

Nebulizer treatment is one of the fastest ways to open up the airways during an asthma flare, an asthma attack, bronchitis, chest tightness, or a persistent wheezy cough. A doctor at Royall Clinic will assess your symptoms, choose the appropriate medication, and supervise the session to make sure you breathe easier and feel better before you leave.

When Nebulizer Treatment Helps

Nebuliser therapy is commonly recommended by our doctor in Klang for the following respiratory conditions and symptoms.

Asthma & Asthma Flare-Ups

Quick relief during asthma symptoms, wheeze, or an asthma attack, with bronchodilator medication delivered straight to the lungs.

Bronchitis & Chest Infections

Open the airways and reduce inflammation in acute or recurring bronchitis, including viral and post-flu chest infections.

Persistent Cough & Wheeze

Soothing relief for stubborn coughs and wheezing, especially when oral medication has not been fast enough.

Bronchiolitis in Infants

Paediatric-sized masks and doses for babies with bronchiolitis, viral wheeze, or noisy breathing under doctor supervision.

Allergic Reactions & Throat Tightness

Calm mild allergic breathing symptoms quickly while we stabilise you and decide on the next step of care.

Post-Viral Breathing Difficulty

Help after a cold, flu, or COVID-19 when breathing still feels tight, congested, or uncomfortable.

Who It's For

  • Adults with asthma, bronchitis, or chest tightness
  • Children with viral wheeze, bronchiolitis, or allergic cough
  • Infants struggling to feed due to blocked or noisy breathing
  • Smokers and ex-smokers with chronic chest symptoms
  • Patients needing fast relief before further investigation

What to Bring & What to Expect

  • Your current inhaler, spacer, or any breathing medication
  • A list of allergies and current medications
  • Prior medical reports or discharge notes, if available
  • Avoid a heavy meal 30 minutes before the session
  • Sit comfortably for 10–20 minutes while the mist is inhaled
  • Reviewed by the doctor afterwards with a clear next-step plan

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about nebulizer treatment at Royall Clinic, Klang.

What is nebulizer treatment?

Nebulizer treatment is a therapy that turns liquid medicine into a fine mist inhaled through a mask or mouthpiece. The mist reaches the airways and lungs directly, providing fast relief from breathing problems such as asthma, cough, and bronchitis.

Who should consider nebulizer treatment in Klang?

Adults and children with asthma, bronchiolitis, bronchitis, allergic cough, persistent wheezing, chest tightness, or sudden breathing difficulty. A doctor will assess you first to confirm the treatment is appropriate.

How long does a nebulizer session take at Royall Clinic?

Most sessions take 10 to 20 minutes, depending on the medication and response. You will be monitored by our clinic staff throughout the session.

Do I need an appointment for nebulizer treatment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead on WhatsApp helps us prepare and reduces waiting time. Please message us if you are bringing a young child or a senior.

Is nebulizer treatment safe for children and babies?

Yes. Paediatric-sized masks and doses are used for children and infants. The treatment is commonly used for bronchiolitis, viral wheeze, and asthma in young children under medical supervision.

How much does nebulizer treatment cost in Klang?

Costs vary with the medication and number of sessions. Royall Clinic provides transparent pricing — message us on WhatsApp for an estimate before your visit.
