Frequently Asked Questions

How can I correct deep hip dips without undergoing an invasive surgery?

Deep hip dips and volume depressions along the lateral gluteal zone can be efficiently filled out using medical-grade body dermal fillers or biocompatible collagen stimulators. These advanced materials smoothly round out structural contours instantly, providing an enhanced silhouette with no post-surgical downtime.

How long do the aesthetic results of a non-surgical buttock enhancement last?

The longevity depends on the precise formulation chosen by our doctor. Hyaluronic acid body fillers typically remain stable for 12 to 18 months, whereas structural collagen stimulators work gradually and can provide beautifully sustained contour support for up to 24 months.

Is there extensive downtime required after a non-surgical gluteal sculpting session?

No structural downtime is required. Clients can walk normally and return to professional desk duties immediately following the procedure. It is recommended to avoid strenuous heavy lower-body weight lifting and hot saunas for the first 3 to 5 days to ensure proper settlement.

Are these body sculpting injectables safe and comfortable?

Yes, your safety is our top priority. We only utilize approved, premium-grade biocompatible materials administered under highly sterile clinic conditions. Local anesthetics are integrated into the process to ensure a thoroughly comfortable and smooth experience.
