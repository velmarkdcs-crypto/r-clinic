How much does a hair transplant cost in Klang?

Pricing varies significantly by the technique used (like FUE) and total follicle graft counts needed. At Royall Clinic, we provide structural, customized pricing maps following a clinical diagnostic checkup.

Are the results from FUE hair transplants permanent?

Yes. FUE hair transplant methods use follicles harvested from secure donor regions (often the back of your head), which are genetically resistant to DHT hair loss hormones, allowing permanent growth.

How long does it take to see results from PRP hair growth therapy?

Many patients note initial reductions in hair shedding within 2 to 3 months. Noticeable thickness transformations and structural density changes generally present after 3 to 6 consecutive clinical cycles.
