Understanding Nasolabial Folds

Often referred to simply as smile lines or laugh lines, nasolabial creases run diagonally from the outer wings of the nose down to the corners of the mouth due to clear biological mechanisms:

  • Malar Fat Pad Descent: Mid-face structural fat pads naturally migrate downwards with gravity over time, creating a heavy overlay on the mouth zone.
  • Dermal Matrix Thinning: Natural collagen and structural elastin reserves diminish significantly, leading to superficial skin wrinkling and tissue laxity.
  • Skeletal Resorption: Gradual, minor bone density loss along the maxilla region deepens the foundational shadow beneath the upper fold lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear, direct answers to common questions regarding nasolabial fold injections and laugh line removal treatments in Klang.

How long do dermal fillers for laugh lines last?

Hyaluronic acid dermal fillers for laugh lines and nasolabial folds typically provide instant volume and smoothing that lasts between 9 to 18 months, depending on the specific filler density and your individual metabolic rate.

Is there any downtime after smile line filler injections at your Klang clinic?

There is virtually zero downtime. Most patients return to daily routines in Klang immediately. Minor temporary swelling, injection site redness, or light bruising may occur but typically resolves completely within 24 to 48 hours.

Are laugh line filler treatments painful?

We prioritize patient comfort by using premium dermal fillers infused with lidocaine (a built-in local anesthetic). Additionally, a medical-grade topical numbing cream is applied to the treatment area prior to injection.

How many ml of dermal filler is typically required for laugh lines?

Most patients require 1 to 2 ml of filler in total to soften both sides smoothly. The precise amount depends entirely on the current depth of your lines and whether we need to structurally lift the cheek base to achieve optimal results.
