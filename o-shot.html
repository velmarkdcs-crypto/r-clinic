How It Works

Regenerative Support for Intimate Wellness

The O-Shot (Orgasm Shot) is a minimally invasive clinical procedure utilizing Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) derived entirely from your own blood to naturally stimulate localized tissue health.

Why Women Choose It

This medical innovation targets several natural anatomical changes associated with childbirth, hormonal shifts, and aging:

  • Alleviating stress urinary incontinence (SUI)
  • Improving overall local tissue sensitivity
  • Enhancing natural hydration and cellular comfort
  • Relieving discomfort during physical intimacy

What To Expect

Our priority is providing a completely supportive, professional clinical environment throughout your entire journey:

Following a blood draw, plasma concentrated with key regenerative factors is precisely isolated. A localized topical numbing block guarantees optimal structural comfort during the brief application process.

Is there any downtime after an O-Shot procedure?

Minimal to no down-time is required. Most patients resume regular daily programmatic schedules immediately, following simple personalized hygiene protocols provided post-consultation.

When do you typically see visible results?

While tissue revitalization begins cellular cycles quickly, early feedback shifts often surface within 3 to 7 days, with complete optimal benefits developing over 2 to 3 months.

Is the clinical procedure painful?

We implement medical-grade local topical desensitization creams prior to the procedure to maintain a highly comfortable, pressure-only sensation environment.
