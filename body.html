Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct insights regarding non-surgical body sculpting and professional slimming procedures in Klang.

How does non-invasive body contouring differ from surgical liposuction?

Non-invasive body contouring eliminates stubborn fat cells using medical technologies like radiofrequency or ultrasound without incisions, anesthesia, or downtime. Liposuction is a surgical procedure requiring downtime and recovery.

How many body slimming sessions are typically required?

Most patients achieve optimal results within 4 to 6 sessions, spaced 2 weeks apart, depending on individual body composition and targeting goals.

Is there any downtime after a skin tightening treatment?

There is zero downtime. You can return to your regular daily activities, including work and exercise, immediately following your clinic session.

Are the results from non-surgical fat reduction permanent?

Yes. Once target fat cells are disrupted and metabolized by the body, they do not return. Maintaining a stable lifestyle and balanced nutrition ensures your newly contoured silhouette remains permanent.
