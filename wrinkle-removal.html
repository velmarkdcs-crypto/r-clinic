Targeted Line Smoothing Methods

Different types of wrinkles require specific clinical actions. We deliver personalized aesthetic treatments to smooth dynamic lines and plump up deep static creases.

Neuromodulator (Botox) Therapy

Botox is a brand of neuromodulator — purified botulinum toxin used to temporarily relax overactive facial muscles. Highly effective for flattening dynamic lines like forehead creases, crow's feet, and stubborn frown lines while preserving natural expression.

Dermal Volume Fillers

Works immediately to fill out deep, resting wrinkles and static folds by replenishing localized volume beneath the skin surface.

Collagen Induction Coils

Utilizes laser or intradermal systems to restore structural firmness, remodeling aging skin and reducing fine lines around fragile tissue zones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding recovery time, longevity, and procedure safety.

Will wrinkle removal treatments make my face look frozen?

No. At Royall Clinic, our philosophy focuses on conservative micro-dosing. We carefully target overactive muscles to smooth out expression lines while fully preserving your natural facial expressions and emotions.

How fast will I notice visible results and how long do they last?

Neuromodulator treatments start showing smoothing adjustments within 3 to 5 days, reaching peak visible refinement at day 14. Results typically maintain their smooth profile for 3 to 6 months depending on individual metabolic rates.

Is there any recovery downtime involved?

Virtually none. This quick walkthrough procedure is often called a "lunchtime treatment." You may notice tiny raised bumps or mild redness at the injection sites, but these naturally disappear within 15 to 30 minutes.
