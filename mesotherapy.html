Why Topical Skincare Isn't Enough

Even premium over-the-counter creams fail to yield profound corrective changes due to structural biological barriers:

  • The Stratum Corneum Barrier: The outer protective skin envelope blocks molecules larger than 500 Daltons, stranding up to 90% of facial serums on the surface.
  • Zero Cellular Activation: Passive topicals cannot penetrate deep enough to feed fibroblasts located in the active dermal layer.
  • Wasted Formulation Potency: Active ingredients oxidize rapidly when exposed to air during daily application, reducing real clinical effectiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear, direct answers regarding professional facial mesotherapy procedures at our Klang clinic.

What is facial mesotherapy and how does it work?

Facial mesotherapy is a non-surgical treatment involving localized micro-injections of a specialized cocktail containing vitamins, amino acids, minerals, and hyaluronic acid directly into the mesoderm (middle layer of the skin). This bypasses the skin barrier to provide intense cellular nourishment and trigger natural rejuvenation.

How many mesotherapy sessions are needed to see a clear result?

While a subtle radiance boost is common within 48 hours of your first treatment, we generally recommend a structured cycle of 3 to 5 sessions spaced roughly 2 to 4 weeks apart to achieve comprehensive, long-term improvements in skin hydration and firmness.

Is there any recovery downtime involved?

Downtime is minimal. You may experience slight redness, minor swelling, or microscopic injection marks at the treated site. These are completely normal signs of cellular stimulation and typically clear up within 24 to 48 hours.

Can mesotherapy help with stubborn pigmentation issues?

Yes. When customized with specific lightening agents like Vitamin C, glutathione, or tranexamic acid, mesotherapy actively inhibits melanin synthesis at a cellular level, helping to fade sun spots and uneven blemishes.
