Ultrasound Scanning Capabilities

We provide localized internal imaging to help diagnose unexplained pain, screen organ function, or observe developmental timelines safely.

Abdominal & Pelvic Scan

Detailed mapping of internal fields including your liver, gallbladder, kidneys, uterus, and ovaries to identify cysts, stones, or fatty tissue changes.

Maternity Screening

Essential early pregnancy monitoring, confirming gestational age, fetal development progress, and heartbeat checks in a supportive care setting.

Soft Tissue (Lumps & Bumps)

Evaluates localized swellings, superficial muscle nodules, or palpable lymph node structures to rule out structural anomalies.

Upper Abdominal Scanning

Requires visibility optimization by preventing digestive air bubbles from blocking clear views of deeper internal structures.

  • Fasting Protocol: Complete fasting for 6 to 8 hours prior to your medical booking is essential.
  • Permitted Fluids: Plain water is allowed to stay clear of dehydration markers. Avoid coffee, dairy, or sweet juices.
  • Organ Checklist: Primarily evaluates structural patterns of the liver, gallbladder, spleen, and renal spaces.

Pelvic & Early Pregnancy Scan

A full bladder acts as a clear sound wave window, displacing bowel structures to display the uterine and ovarian fields clearly.

  • Hydration Benchmark: Drink 3 to 4 glasses of water 1 hour before your scan session. Do not void.
  • Fasting Requirements: No dietary restrictions apply for pelvic-only pelvic scans.
  • Organ Checklist: Evaluates uterine lines, ovarian volumes, endometrial thickness, or early gestational tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to clear queries regarding diagnostic medical scans in Klang.

Do I need to fast before an ultrasound scan?

Fasting requirements depend entirely on the type of scan. For an upper abdomen ultrasound (evaluating the gallbladder, liver, or pancreas), fasting for 6 to 8 hours is mandatory. For pelvic or early pregnancy scans, fasting is not required, but you must have a full bladder.

How long does a pelvic or abdominal ultrasound scan take?

A standard medical ultrasound assessment takes approximately 15 to 30 minutes, depending on the complexity of the symptoms being analyzed.

Will I get my ultrasound imaging results on the same day?

Yes. At Royall Clinic, our general practitioners review and explain your screening scans on the spot during your consultation session.

Can I check for kidney stones or gallstones with this scan?

Yes, diagnostic abdominal and pelvic scans are highly effective for detecting gallstones, kidney anomalies, urinary bladder blocks, or fatty liver changes.
