Comprehensive Birth Control Options in Klang

We provide medical guidance and smooth procedures across all major short-acting and long-acting modern contraception variations available in Malaysia.

Birth Control Pills & Injections

Accurate prescriptions for combined oral contraceptive pills (COCP) or progestogen-only alternatives. We also administer highly reliable three-monthly contraceptive injections to safeguard your cycle.

Contraceptive Implants (Implanon)

A small, flexible rod placed just under the skin of your inner upper arm. Implanon continuously releases progestogen to prevent pregnancy for up to 3 full years, offering over 99% real-world effectiveness.

Intrauterine Devices (IUCD)

Highly effective non-hormonal copper intrauterine devices or hormonal equivalents inserted cleanly inside the uterus by our clinical staff. IUCDs provide long-term protection spanning 3 to 5 years.

Emergency Contraception & Family Planning

Timing matters significantly in emergency reproductive healthcare. We offer confidential, time-sensitive options alongside regular structural mapping.

  • Post-coital emergency pills ("morning-after pills") to delay ovulation safely.
  • Emergency copper intrauterine device insertion parameters within 120 hours.
  • Accurate, rapid in-clinic beta-hCG blood testing for definitive timeline detection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding local family planning options and clinical procedures.

How long does it take to insert a contraceptive implant (Implanon)?

The insertion takes under 5 minutes. The doctor numbs a small area on your inner arm using local anesthesia, ensuring you feel zero pain while the rod is safely guided under the surface of the skin.

What is the difference between an IUCD and a birth control pill?

An Intrauterine Device (IUCD) is a long-acting, set-and-forget option lasting 3 to 5 years inserted by a medical expert. Birth control pills are short-acting daily micro-dosed tablets that rely strictly on daily routine to stay effective.

Can I reverse my contraception choice if I decide to conceive?

Yes. Both contraceptive implants and copper or hormonal IUCDs are completely reversible. Your natural ovulation cycle patterns recover very rapidly once the devices are extracted by our doctors.
