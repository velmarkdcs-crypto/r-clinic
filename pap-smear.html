Preparation Guidelines

To help ensure sample collection efficiency and ensure testing accuracy, please plan your visit keeping these instructions in mind:

  • Book your screening slot roughly 10 to 14 days following the first day of your period.
  • Avoid using vaginal treatments, washes, or spermicidal gels for 48 hours prior to testing.
  • Refrain from sexual activity for 48 hours before your visit to keep surface samples clear.
  • Ensure any active pelvic infections are fully treated prior to arranging your checkup.
