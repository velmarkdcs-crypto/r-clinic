Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered clearly to help you make an informed choice.

What is a non-surgical energy facelift?

A non-surgical energy facelift uses advanced energy-based systems (such as radiofrequency, ultrasound, or laser) to deliver controlled heat into the deeper structural layers of the skin. This triggers immediate tissue contraction and stimulates fresh collagen production, gradually lifting and firming the face over the following weeks — all without incisions, anesthesia, or extended downtime.

How long do the results of a non-surgical facelift last?

Most patients enjoy visible lifting and tightening for 12 to 24 months, depending on their age, skin condition, lifestyle, and the specific protocol used. Periodic maintenance sessions are recommended to sustain optimal results over the long term.

Is there any downtime after the treatment?

Non-surgical facelift protocols typically involve minimal downtime. You may experience mild redness, swelling, or warmth in the treated area for 24 to 72 hours. Most patients resume normal daily activities almost immediately after the procedure.

Who is an ideal candidate for a non-surgical facelift?

Anyone with mild to moderate skin laxity, early jowling, or soft tissue descent who wants a refreshed, lifted appearance without surgery. It is also an excellent option for patients who want to postpone or avoid invasive procedures. Our doctors will assess your facial anatomy during consultation to confirm suitability.
