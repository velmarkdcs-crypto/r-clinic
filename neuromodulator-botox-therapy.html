Frequently Asked Questions

Will my face look frozen?

Not when performed conservatively by an experienced clinician. The goal is to soften lines without losing natural expression.

Is there downtime?

Most patients resume normal activities immediately. Minor redness or small bumps may settle within a few hours.

Is Botox good for forehead lines and frown lines?

Yes. Botox is widely used for forehead lines and frown line reduction because it relaxes the underlying muscles that cause repeated creasing.

How soon will I see the result?

Initial smoothing may be seen within 3 to 5 days, with the full result visible around 1 to 2 weeks post-treatment.
