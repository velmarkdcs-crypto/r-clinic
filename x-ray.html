Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our radiology workflows and diagnostic timelines.

Do I need a doctor's referral to get an X-ray at Royall Clinic?

While having a referral letter from an outside GP or specialist is helpful, it is not mandatory. You can walk in or schedule an appointment; our in-house medical doctor will briefly consult with you to confirm the necessary imaging views for your safety and medical accuracy.

How long does it take to get my X-ray results and medical report?

The digital image itself is generated almost instantly after the scan. A preliminary review will be discussed during your consultation, and a structured, certified radiologist report is typically completed and delivered within the same day or the following working day.

Is an appointment required for an X-ray imaging session?

Walk-ins are welcome for urgent medical assessments such as sudden trauma or suspected minor fractures. However, booking an appointment via WhatsApp is highly recommended to eliminate long waiting times and ensure our radiographer is readily available.

Are digital X-rays safe, and what about radiation exposure?

Yes, our clinic uses modern digital X-ray equipment which emits significantly lower doses of radiation compared to older, traditional film machines. We also use standardized lead shielding protocols to protect non-targeted areas of the body.
