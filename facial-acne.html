Targeted Acne Treatment Modalities

Acne isn't a one-size-fits-all issue. Based on your skin's specific condition—whether it is hormonal, cystic, or comedonal—we utilize clinical protocols designed to clear skin safely.

Deep Pore Cleansing

Targeted deep-cleansing protocol that reaches compacted sebum and debris lodged within the lower follicular ducts, decongesting pores and reducing the formation of deep, painful cystic lesions.

Superficial Pore Treatment

Gentle, surface-level extraction and refinement of open comedones and micro-impactions to instantly smooth skin texture, refine enlarged pores, and restore a clean, even complexion.

Subcision Method

A minor medical procedure that releases fibrotic strands tethering rolling acne scars to underlying tissue, stimulating natural collagen regeneration to lift and smooth atrophic scar depressions.

Understanding Acne in Malaysia's Climate

High humidity and consistent tropical temperatures in Klang can significantly increase sebum (oil) production, turning trapped sweat and pollution into a breeding ground for acne-causing bacteria (P. acnes).

Our clinical treatments focus on adjusting your skin's oil-to-moisture ratios while eliminating underlying bacterial inflammation, building long-term skin resilience against future flare-ups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding professional acne care at Royall Clinic.

How long does it take to see results from professional acne treatment?

While dynamic inflammation and superficial congestion often improve within the first 2 to 3 weeks, deeper cellular turnover and substantial clearing of severe acne generally require a dedicated 8 to 12-week custom program.

Why should I choose a medical clinic over a normal beauty salon facial?

Medical clinics offer regulated therapeutic ingredients, sterile medical-grade extraction procedures, and doctor-led care. This approach treats acne as a clinical skin condition, avoiding common beauty salon pitfalls like over-scrubbing that can worsen active infection.

Will facial acne treatments leave deep scars on my skin?

No. When performed correctly under medical oversight, clinical extractions relieve internal pressure safely. It is actually squeezed at-home popping that ruptures the inner cell walls, creating permanent acne scarring.

What type of facial acne is most common in Klang, Selangor?

Due to high humidity in Klang, comedonal acne (blackheads and whiteheads) and inflammatory papules are incredibly common. Excess humidity triggers hyper-seborrhea, which blocks follicular openings when combined with dead skin cells.
