FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear medical answers about regenerative and functional care, stem cell, exosome, and NAD+ therapies at Royall Clinic in Klang.

What is regenerative and functional care?

Regenerative and functional care focuses on supporting the body's natural healing, cellular renewal, and long-term vitality. It uses targeted therapies such as stem cell, exosome, and NAD+ protocols to address root causes of fatigue, ageing, and recovery, rather than only managing symptoms.

What is stem cell therapy used for?

Stem cell therapy is used at Royall Clinic to support tissue repair, joint health, skin rejuvenation, hair restoration, and overall cellular renewal. Our doctor evaluates each patient's goals and medical history to determine the appropriate stem cell protocol.

How does exosome therapy work?

Exosomes are nano-sized vesicles that carry growth factors, peptides, and mRNA between cells. When delivered through injections or IV, exosomes signal surrounding cells to repair, regenerate, and function more efficiently, supporting skin quality, energy, and recovery.

What does NAD+ IV therapy do?

NAD+ is a coenzyme essential for mitochondrial energy production, DNA repair, and cellular metabolism. NAD+ IV therapy replenishes declining levels to support energy, mental clarity, recovery, and healthy ageing. Most patients report noticeable benefits within days of treatment.

Are these therapies safe?

Yes. All regenerative and functional care protocols at Royall Clinic are administered by licensed medical doctors under strict sterile conditions. Each treatment begins with a full medical assessment to confirm safety, suitability, and optimal expected outcomes.

How many sessions are required?

The number of sessions depends on the protocol, your goals, and your body's response. Many patients begin noticing benefits from a single NAD+ IV session, while stem cell and exosome programmes typically involve an initial course of 2-3 sessions followed by maintenance as advised by our doctor.
