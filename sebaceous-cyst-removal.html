Why Choose Royall Clinic for Cyst Excision in Klang?

Sebaceous cysts (often confused with epidermoid cysts) are non-cancerous sacs under the skin containing keratin or oily material. While they are usually harmless, they can become inflamed, infected, painful, or grow larger over time. Attempting to squeeze or pop them at home often causes deep infections and recurrence. Our medical doctors perform precise, minor surgical excisions to remove the entire cyst wall, minimizing the chance of it ever returning.

When Should You Consider Removal?

You should have your cyst evaluated for minor surgery if you observe any of the following symptoms:

  • The cyst grows rapidly or becomes highly noticeable.
  • It shows signs of infection (redness, warmth, tenderness, or leaking pus).
  • It is constantly irritated by clothing, shaving, or movement.
  • Cosmetic concerns due to locations on the face, neck, scalp, or torso.

Comprehensive Post-Care Support

Frequently Asked Questions About Cyst Removal

Clear answers to common questions about our minor surgery services in Klang.

Does sebaceous cyst removal hurt?

The procedure is highly comfortable. We use a local anesthetic to completely numb the area prior to starting the excision, meaning you will feel little to no pain while the doctor works.

Will my cyst grow back after surgery?

By opting for a complete surgical excision rather than a simple drainage, our doctors extract the full underlying sac wall. This significantly drops the structural chances of the cyst reforming.

How long does the minor surgery take?

Most in-office cyst removals are completed within 30 to 45 minutes, depending directly on the overall size and current location of the skin lump.

Is there any down time after the procedure?

There is minimal downtime. Most patients return to light activities or work immediately. However, we advise avoiding heavy lifting or strenuous exercise until your sutures are safely removed.
