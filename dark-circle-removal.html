Understanding the Root Cause of Dark Circles

An effective under-eye treatment begins with an accurate diagnosis. Dark circles are rarely a one-size-fits-all issue, and are generally categorized into three key underlying factors.

1. Structural Shadows

As we age, we naturally lose volume in the mid-face area. This creates a deep hollow beneath the eye—commonly known as a tear trough—casting a shadow that mimics dark pigment.

2. Hyperpigmentation

An overproduction of melanin under the eyes creates a brownish or dark patch. This is frequently triggered by genetic factors, sun exposure, or consistent friction from rubbing the eyes.

3. Vascular Congestion

The skin beneath our eyes is the thinnest on the body. When micro-circulation slows down due to allergies, lack of sleep, or aging, bluish-purple blood vessels show right through the surface.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many treatment sessions will I need?

It depends entirely on the cause. Structural volume treatments like fillers yield immediate results after just one session. Pigmentation-focused treatments such as lasers or mesotherapy generally require 3 to 6 sessions spaced out over a few weeks for complete, long-term improvement.

Are the procedures painful?

Comfort is our absolute priority. We apply high-strength topical numbing cream before any injectable or laser procedure. Most clients experience little to no pain, describing the sensation as a light tingling or mild pressure.

Is there any downtime afterward?

Most of our under-eye options involve minimal to zero downtime. You can return to your regular daily activities right away. Minor temporary swelling or faint pinprick bruising can sometimes happen with injections, but this usually fades cleanly over 3 to 5 days.
