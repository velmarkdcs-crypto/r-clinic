Our Medical Removal Methods

Depending on the location, base size, and thickness of your skin tags, our doctor utilizes precise minor surgical and clinical tools to eliminate growths smoothly.

Electrocautery

Uses localized electrical thermal energy to precisely seal the stalk blood vessels and burn away the skin tag tissue instantly, minimized bleeding risk.

Surgical Excision

Ideal for larger or pedunculated tags. Performed cleanly under local anaesthesia using sterile surgical instruments with clean cosmetic edge-work.

Cryotherapy

Utilizes ultra-low temperature liquid nitrogen targeting to freeze the core structure, causing the benign growth to naturally drop off within days.
