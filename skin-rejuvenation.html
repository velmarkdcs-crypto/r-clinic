FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear medical and aesthetic answers regarding our advanced skin renewal protocols.

How many sessions will I need to see visible results?

While a visible "glow" and improved hydration can often be seen within a week of your first treatment, optimal corrective results for pigment or scarring typically require a tailored program of 3 to 6 sessions, spaced 4 weeks apart.

Is there any downtime associated with skin rejuvenation?

Downtime depends entirely on the modality used. Gentle medical peels and meso-infusions have zero downtime—just minor flushness for a few hours. Deeper fractional laser resurfacing may result in mild redness and slight peeling for 3 to 5 days.

What should I do for aftercare following a treatment?

The most crucial rule is protecting your fresh skin cells: apply a broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen daily and avoid direct sun exposure for at least one week. Keep the skin hydrated with a gentle, fragrance-free barrier cream and skip active acids (like Retinol or Vitamin C) for 5 days.
