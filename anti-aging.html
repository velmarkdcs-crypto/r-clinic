Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our anti-aging protocols, regenerative therapies, and what to expect at Royall Clinic in Klang.

At what age should I start anti-aging treatments?

Preventive anti-aging care can begin in the mid-20s to early 30s, focusing on collagen preservation, sun protection, and skin quality. Corrective treatments for visible lines, volume loss, and pigmentation typically become more relevant from the late 30s onward. Our doctors tailor plans to your skin's actual condition rather than age alone.

Are collagen stimulation and biomodulator injections safe?

Yes. Both are doctor-administered, clinically proven procedures using regulated, medical-grade products. Our team evaluates your medical history, skin type, and treatment goals before recommending either option, ensuring protocols are appropriate and safe for you.

How do exosome and stem cell therapies differ?

Exosome therapy uses cell-signalling messengers (growth factors, peptides, mRNA) to accelerate repair, brighten skin, and improve texture with minimal downtime. Stem cell therapy works at a deeper regenerative level, supporting long-term tissue repair and dermal renewal. Our doctors will recommend the most suitable approach—or a combination—based on your concerns.

How long do the results last?

Result longevity depends on the modality, your skin condition, and lifestyle. Collagen stimulation and biomodulators typically last 4–6 months, while regenerative therapies (exosomes, stem cell) can deliver progressive improvements lasting 6–12 months. Maintenance sessions are recommended to sustain results.

Is there any downtime after the treatments?

Most anti-aging protocols involve little to no downtime. You may experience mild redness, swelling, or pinpoint bruising at injection sites for 24–72 hours. Our medical team provides clear post-treatment care instructions to ensure optimal healing and results.

Can anti-aging treatments be combined with other procedures?

Yes. Many patients benefit from a multi-modality plan that combines collagen stimulation, biomodulators, and skin-quality therapies for comprehensive rejuvenation. Our doctors will design a sequenced protocol to safely layer treatments for synergistic, natural-looking results.
