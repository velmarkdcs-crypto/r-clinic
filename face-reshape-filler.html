Dermal Filler FAQ

Essential expert facts before choosing facial reshaping treatments in Klang.

How long do face reshaping dermal fillers typically last?

Because high-density structural fillers are used for chin and jaw modifications, they break down slowly. Results routinely persist anywhere from 12 to 18 months depending on individual metabolic profiles.

Is the face reshape filler injection painful?

Discomfort is minimal. We employ prescription-grade external numbing creams prior to starting, and our selected premium filler gels are mixed directly with lidocaine to maximize comfort throughout the injection steps.

What is the difference between dermal fillers and wrinkle-relaxing injections?

Dermal fillers use a gel compound to fill up sunken spaces and expand structural bone borders. In contrast, wrinkle-reducing options temporarily relax specific facial muscles to smooth out superficial dynamic fine lines.
