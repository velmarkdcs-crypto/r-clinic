Overcome Stubborn Viral Warts Permanently

Warts are localized skin infections caused by the Human Papillomavirus (HPV) family. They manifest as rough, grainy bumps on the hands, fingers, or soles of the feet (plantar warts). Because warts are highly contagious, scratching or picking at them can easily spread the virus to other parts of your body or to family members. Over-the-counter peeling kits are often slow or cause chemical burns on healthy tissue. Our clinic provides reliable medical interventions that break down infected tissue cleanly and quickly.

Wart Types We Treat in Clinic

Our doctors evaluate and clear several classes of skin warts, including:

  • Common Warts: Rough, raised bumps typically multiplying around hands, fingers, and elbows.
  • Plantar Warts (Verrucas): Hard, deeply embedded growths on heels or soles that cause sharp pain while walking.
  • Periungual Warts: Persistent growths forming beneath or closely around fingernails and toenails.

Post-Treatment Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions About Wart Removal

Reliable medical insights regarding viral wart management at Royall Clinic.

What is the difference between a wart and a corn?

Warts are caused by a viral infection and show tiny, pinpoint black dots (clotted blood vessels) under close inspection. Corns are non-viral thickenings of skin caused by repetitive friction or pressure from ill-fitting shoes.

Can multiple warts be removed in a single visit?

Yes. If you have several warts on your hands, feet, or body, our doctors can address multiple lesions in a single session to save you time and halt further spread.

Is there downtime after getting a plantar wart removed?

You can walk immediately after the session, though deep plantar warts on the sole of the foot may cause minor tenderness for a few days. We provide protective cushioning dressings to keep you comfortable.

Why do warts sometimes return after removal?

Warts are viral. If virus particles remain dormant in the surrounding skin layers, a wart can grow back. This is why professional, doctor-led removal is more effective than superficial home treatments.
