Common Symptoms

  • Leathery, heavily lined patches of skin (Lichenification)
  • Constant, localized itching spikes during rest
  • Raised, rough lesions primarily on ankles, neck, wrists, or forearms
  • Hyperpigmentation from ongoing chronic rubbing damage

Understanding the Condition

Lichen Simplex Chronicus is not primary skin failure; it is an integrated physiological response to consistent micro-trauma from scratching. Often triggered by prolonged anxiety, insect bites, or clothing friction, the skin defense network over-compensates by building dense, leathery protective plaques.

At Royall Clinic Klang, our clinical target focuses heavily on interrupting this neurological cycle through targeted chemical and physical interventions.
