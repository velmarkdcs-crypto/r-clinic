Frequently Asked Questions

Get reliable clinical context regarding fertility testing timelines and common diagnostic screenings.

When should a couple consider undergoing an infertility screening?

Clinical guidelines generally suggest a fertility review if a couple has been trying to conceive naturally for over 12 months without success (or over 6 months if the female partner is 35 years or older).

What conditions must be met before a semen analysis test?

For an accurate reading, the male partner should maintain strict sexual abstinence for 2 to 5 days before sample collection. This step ensures optimal sperm volume, count, and active motility measurements during evaluation.

How does an AMH blood test help evaluate female fertility?

An Anti-Müllerian Hormone (AMH) test measures hormone levels produced by early-stage ovarian follicles. It provides a helpful indication of a woman's remaining egg count (ovarian reserve) without requiring invasive procedures.
