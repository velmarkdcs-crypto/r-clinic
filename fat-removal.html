Frequently Asked Questions

Essential medical facts regarding modern double chin and face fat options in Klang.

Are the results of non-surgical face fat reduction permanent?

Yes, because the human body maintains a stable count of fat cells during adulthood. When these localized fat structures are cleared by lipolytic methods or ultrasound applications, they are gone for good. Maintaining a steady weight ensures your remaining cells do not swell.

How many sessions are typically required to clear a double chin?

While many individuals notice a visible reduction after their first couple of visits, a structured schedule of 2 to 4 micro-sessions spaced 4 weeks apart is generally recommended to achieve deep, sharp jawline separation.

Will my skin sag after the facial fat is eliminated?

Our advanced protocols combine fat-slimming technologies with built-in tissue-tightening modes (such as HIFU energy). This ensures that as the submental volume reduces, the overlying skin contracts firmly against your jaw structure rather than hanging loose.
