Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Royall Clinic handles personal data, including information shared with our WhatsApp AI receptionist and the service providers that support it.

Please avoid sending detailed medical records, identity documents, payment details, or information that is not needed for your enquiry through WhatsApp. The AI receptionist is an administrative service; it does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or replace a consultation with a healthcare professional.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Royall Clinic (Klinik Royall) is responsible for personal data collected for clinic enquiries and appointment coordination. Our website identifies the clinic as managed by NOVAXEL SDN BHD (SSM: 202001015228 (1371548-U)).

Technology provider: Velmark Digital Care Solutions provides technology services supporting the WhatsApp AI receptionist. It acts as a service provider in connection with that system; Royall Clinic remains the point of contact for requests about your personal data.

2. Personal data we handle

Depending on what you choose to send or tell us, the receptionist and related workflows may handle:

  • Your name and WhatsApp phone number, including the number associated with your WhatsApp account.
  • The text and other content you send, our replies, and relevant conversation details.
  • Treatment or service enquiries, including information you volunteer about symptoms, health concerns, or medical history.
  • Appointment requests and related details such as your preferred date or time and the service you are asking about.
  • Technical information needed to deliver the service, such as message, delivery, or website request metadata, where made available through the relevant provider.

Health information is sensitive personal data. Please share only what is necessary to help us direct your enquiry. Do not use the AI receptionist for emergencies; contact emergency services or seek urgent medical care.

3. Why we use your data

We use the information above to respond to questions about the clinic and its treatments, understand and route enquiries to clinic staff, process and follow up on appointment requests, and operate, maintain, and improve the receptionist and its workflows. We may also use relevant information to meet legal or regulatory obligations and to protect the clinic, patients, and service.

Providing information is your choice. If you do not provide details needed to respond or coordinate a booking, we may be unable to complete that request through the receptionist. You can contact the clinic directly instead.

4. WhatsApp AI receptionist and service providers

When you message the clinic on WhatsApp, your message and contact details are handled through the WhatsApp service and the configured systems used to deliver the receptionist. The workflow may use the following providers for these functions:

  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API: carries WhatsApp messages and related delivery information between you and the clinic’s connected business messaging service. Meta processes data under its own terms and privacy practices.
  • n8n: automates the configured workflow, such as passing an incoming enquiry to the AI service, routing a response, or sending appointment-request details to the relevant record or clinic staff.
  • Groq AI: processes message text and relevant prompt context to generate an automated, conversational response. The AI may misunderstand a message; staff may need to confirm information or handle a request.
  • Google Sheets: may be used in the configured workflow to organise or record appointment requests and enquiry details for clinic follow-up.
  • Cloudflare: may provide website or network-delivery services, such as routing and protection. Where a request passes through Cloudflare, technical connection and request information may be processed as part of providing those services.

Information is shared with these providers as needed for their service functions. Each provider may process information under its own terms, privacy documentation, and service configuration. The provider’s involvement does not make an AI response a clinical assessment. For diagnosis, treatment decisions, or confidential clinical care, speak directly with a clinic healthcare professional.

5. Healthcare privacy and confidentiality

We treat health-related enquiries as sensitive and handle them with care. WhatsApp and the supporting technology providers are separate services with their own systems and privacy terms; a message sent there is not the same as a private consultation with a doctor. Do not send information you are uncomfortable sharing through these services.

Only share what is relevant to your request. If your enquiry requires a clinical discussion, clinic staff can direct you to an appropriate channel or appointment. The AI receptionist is not intended to create or replace a medical record, and you should not rely on it for medical decisions.

6. Sharing and transfers

We may make relevant information available to authorised clinic staff who need it to respond to or coordinate your request, and to the technology providers described above to operate the service. We may also disclose data where required or permitted by applicable law.

Because the service uses third-party platforms, information may be processed or accessed in locations outside Malaysia, depending on each provider’s infrastructure and configuration. Applicable Malaysian personal data transfer requirements continue to apply. Please consult the providers’ current privacy information for details of their own processing.

7. How long information is kept

We retain personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this notice, or as required or permitted by applicable law. The retention period can depend on the type of information, the reason it was collected, clinic recordkeeping needs, and the settings and retention practices of the service providers. We do not state a fixed retention period here. You may ask us about information relating to you.

8. Protecting your information

Royall Clinic and its service providers use administrative and technical measures appropriate to their respective services and responsibilities. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be represented as completely secure. Please take care when choosing what to send over WhatsApp, and tell the clinic promptly if you believe your information has been sent to the wrong recipient or otherwise exposed.

9. Malaysian data protection law

Royall Clinic handles personal data in accordance with Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709), including amendments made by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024, to the extent applicable and in force. The Act’s requirements include principles concerning notice and choice, disclosure, security, retention, data integrity, and access. Health information may constitute sensitive personal data and is subject to additional requirements under the Act.

This notice describes the purposes and general categories of processing for the receptionist. Where consent is required, we will seek it in accordance with applicable law. If you have concerns about how your information is handled, contact Royall Clinic using the details below. You may also refer to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia.

10. Your choices and data rights

Subject to the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and other applicable requirements, you may:

  • Request access to personal data about you that we hold.
  • Request correction of personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete, misleading, or not up to date.
  • Ask us to stop or limit processing where the law provides a right to do so, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Ask us to delete or erase information. We will assess the request under applicable law and may need to retain some information where required or permitted for clinic, legal, or recordkeeping purposes. Deletion may not remove copies held independently by WhatsApp or other providers; you may need to contact them as well.
  • Where applicable, exercise any additional rights available under the Act, including data portability rights introduced by amendments, subject to statutory conditions.

To protect your privacy, we may need to verify your identity and clarify the information or request. We will respond in accordance with applicable legal requirements. Some requests may affect our ability to continue a conversation or process an appointment request.

11. Children’s information

A parent or legal guardian should contact the clinic on behalf of a child and should not provide more information than is necessary for the enquiry. Please do not use the AI receptionist for urgent care or to share extensive medical details about a child.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our practices, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page. Please review it periodically, especially before using the WhatsApp AI receptionist.

13. Contact Royall Clinic

For an access, correction, deletion, or other privacy request, contact the clinic directly. Please mark your request “Privacy / Personal Data Request” and describe what you need without including unnecessary medical details.
