Featured Treatment

P-Shot (Priapus Shot) - Regenerative PRP Therapy

The P-Shot is our most-requested men's wellness procedure. It uses Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) derived from your own blood to support penile tissue health, circulation, and intimate wellness. Non-surgical, drug-free, and performed by licensed medical doctors.

By concentrating the natural growth factors found in your own platelets, the P-Shot delivers targeted regenerative support to the tissues responsible for vascular health and function. Because the PRP is autologous (sourced from your own body), the procedure is biocompatible and carries minimal risk of adverse reaction.

Our clinic provides a thorough medical assessment, a clear explanation of expected benefits and limitations, and a personalised aftercare plan to support optimal outcomes.

The Clinical Pathway

Your P-Shot Treatment Journey

Our structured four-step process ensures your safety, comfort, and clarity from consultation to follow-up care.

Step 01

Private Consultation

A confidential medical review of your health history, current medications, and personal wellness goals to confirm suitability for the P-Shot.

Step 02

PRP Preparation

A small blood sample is drawn and processed in a medical-grade centrifuge to isolate the platelet-rich plasma concentrate.

Step 03

Targeted Injection

The concentrated PRP is carefully injected into specific penile tissues under local anaesthesia by our experienced medical doctor.

Step 04

Aftercare & Follow-Up

Detailed aftercare instructions, supportive follow-up, and optional review appointments to monitor your regenerative progress.
