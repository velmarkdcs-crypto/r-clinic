When to Seek an ECG?

Do not dismiss early warnings. Baseline heart evaluations are essential if you experience:

  • Sudden or recurring chest pain and pressure
  • Unexplained shortness of breath (dyspnea)
  • Flutters or thumping feelings in your chest (palpitations)
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness, or sudden fainting episodes
  • Routine medical examinations for high-risk profiles

What is an ECG test and how does it evaluate heart health?

An Electrocardiogram (ECG) is a quick, completely safe, and non-invasive procedure that records the baseline electrical impulses generated by your heart muscle. Medical practitioners utilize this tracking data to analyze individual heart rhythm rhythms, pinpoint underlying rate irregularities, identify signs of poor blood circulation, or diagnose enlargement anomalies within different cardiac chambers.

Who should actively get an ECG heart checkup in Klang?

An ECG screening is recommended if you have symptoms like acute chest pain, persistent shortness of breath, unexplained vertigo, or hard thumping heart sensations. It is also advised for adults over age 40, individuals with high blood pressure, elevated cholesterol levels, diabetes, or those with a close family background of coronary cardiovascular disease.

How should I properly prepare for my upcoming clinical ECG appointment?

No intensive pre-test fasting rules are required before a standard resting ECG checkup. It is highly recommended to wear a loose-fitting or two-piece outfit that lets our healthcare provider place the testing sensors onto your chest, wrist, and ankle skin easily. Avoid applying thick oily lotions, rich body moisturizers, or heavy skin treatments on test day.
